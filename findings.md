# Key Findings



## Finding 1 - Fraud Rate

The fraud rate is approximately 0.17%, indicating that fraudulent transactions are extremely rare compared to genuine transactions.



## Finding 2 -Average Transaction Amount

Fraudulent transactions have a higher average transaction amount than genuine transactions.



## Finding 3 -High-Value Transactions

High-value transactions contribute disproportionately to the total fraud exposure.



## Finding 4 -Time-Based Fraud

Fraud incidents are concentrated within specific time buckets, suggesting opportunities for time-based monitoring.



# Recommendations

* Monitor high-value transactions in real time.
* Implement time-based fraud detection rules.
* Prioritize alerts based on transaction amount and behavioural patterns.
* Continuously monitor fraud trends to minimize financial losses while reducing false positives.
